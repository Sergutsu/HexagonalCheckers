Three sides.
Six directions.

Checkers, with a little more room to think.

01

Make your move

A pawn moves to any adjacent empty hex, in any of the six directions.

02

Jump to capture

Hop over an opponent into an empty hex to take their pawn. Captures are mandatory. If another jump is available, keep going with the same pawn.

03

Find your superpower

Land on the central hex or any outer-edge hex to upgrade. Superpawns glide any distance along the six directions, and can capture one opponent along a clear ray.

04

Be the last color standing

Capture both opposing armies to win. Empty or blocked sides are skipped. If no color can move, the match is a draw.
