Reversi territory meets hex checkers.

01

Take one action

Every turn you either drop a new piece or move one of yours. Never both.

02

Drop to flip

Place a piece on any empty hex. Every enemy run trapped between it and another of your pieces flips to your colour, in all six directions at once.

03

Move to capture

Step to an adjacent empty hex, or jump an adjacent enemy into the empty hex beyond. The jumped piece is removed for good. Men move forward only.

04

Crown on the far edge

A man that walks or jumps onto an edge hex outside its home edge becomes a King. Kings move and jump in all six directions. Drops never crown.

05

Win the board

Last colour with pieces wins. In a quick match, the first colour to hold all seven central hexes wins. If the board fills, or 40 turns pass without a drop or capture, the most pieces wins.
